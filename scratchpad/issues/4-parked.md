Closing out the Impeccable roadmap: three of its Phase 2 passes were deferred
"until a concrete need appears", and no need has appeared. Recording them here
so the deferral is a decision on the record rather than something lost with the
roadmap file.

- **`delight`** — personality and memorable touches. Broadsheet's register is
  deliberately restrained and hall-first; a delight pass would need a specific
  moment that has earned it.
- **A data-viz pass on `07-data-viz.css`** — the house charts (`.barchart`,
  `.share-bars`) were reviewed in passing during Pass 6 (the column chart keeps
  its `height` animation on purpose, so each bar's value numeral rides up with
  the fill) but never got a dedicated pass.
- **`extract`** — pulling reusable tokens and components into the design system.
  The theme is already modular and documented in DESIGN.md with a
  machine-readable sidecar, so there is nothing obviously stranded.

Deliberately **not** on the roadmap and not deferred: `bolder` / `quieter`
(Broadsheet's register is calibrated; changing it is a new-work decision, not a
dial), `overdrive`, `onboard`, and `craft` (a deprecated alias).

Re-open a specific pass when a real deck gives it a reason.
